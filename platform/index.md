# DAO membership, accounts and community workspaces | Daclify

> Bring members, roles, voting and shared funds together in Daclify. Learn about DAO accounts, the discovery Hub and shared or independent deployments.

https://daclify.com/platform/

Daclify gives your DAO a shared workspace for people, decisions, money and knowledge. Your community sets the rules; members can understand where they fit and what they can do.

## Make participation straightforward.

Daclify accounts identify members inside the smart contract. A member does not need a separate native blockchain account to take part. Roles define who can vote, review work or manage DAO settings.

### An account for each member

Use one identity across your DAO’s tools. Membership and permissions follow the person, instead of being recreated separately for voting, work and documents.

### Keys you control

User-controlled accounts use a local encrypted vault and recovery credentials. Keep your backup safe: signing in on a new device does not replace a lost recovery credential.

### More ways to sign in — On the roadmap

Native wallet linking, social login, Telegram and clearly labelled managed recovery are on the roadmap. These options will have explicit permissions and recovery responsibilities.


## Your DAO keeps its own identity.

Shared contracts give each DAO its own members, settings and treasury records. The Hub provides a place to discover and open those workspaces.

### Shared infrastructure

Create a DAO within a shared deployment and configure the tools your community needs. Keep the organization’s records together in one workspace.

### Independent infrastructure — On the roadmap

The roadmap includes DAO-owned contracts connected to the Hub. Communities choosing this mode will also take responsibility for deployment, upgrades and ongoing operations.


## Voting power and money have different jobs.

Governance credits help your DAO decide who has a say. Treasury assets pay for the work the community supports. Keeping them separate makes the rules easier to explain.

- Define voting power through your DAO’s governance policy.
- Use supported blockchain assets for treasury funding and payments.
- Check the app’s guides for supported tokens and voting policies.

## Learn in the place where you work.

The app includes a searchable handbook for accounts, DAO setup, voting, projects, payroll and documents. Guides show their versions so you can check that the instructions match your connected deployment.

- Use the handbook to understand a feature before taking action.
- Review a module’s permissions before enabling it.
- Keep signing and document recovery credentials safe.
